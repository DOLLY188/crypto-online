AI Stock Intelligence

An AI-powered stock market analysis and prediction project.

Project Goals

- Monitor 50–66 stocks.
- Analyze historical price and volume data.
- Calculate technical indicators.
- Train machine learning models.
- Rank stocks by potential opportunities.
- Provide BUY, WATCH, or AVOID signals.
- Backtest and paper trade the system.
